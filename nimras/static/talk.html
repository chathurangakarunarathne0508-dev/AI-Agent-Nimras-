One-on-one with your AI

This is a quiet space to just talk with Nimras and get a feel for how the model thinks — no files, no reports, just conversation. Ask it anything, including about itself.

  • It runs entirely on your computer. Nothing you type here leaves your machine (unless web search is on).
  • It thinks by predicting text, not by looking up facts — so it can sound confident and still be wrong. Double-check anything important.
  • Internet search is on (the 🌐 button, top right). When a question needs facts or current info, Nimras looks it up on the web and cites sources; turn it off to keep answers fully local.
  • It learns from you through 👍 / 👎 and “remember that…”, but the model itself is never retrained.
Nimras can be wrong — it's a small model running on your PC. Press Enter to send, Shift+Enter for a new line.
